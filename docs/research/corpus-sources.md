# Sources for a corpus of Kazakh official business language (іс қағаздарын жүргізу)

**Compiled:** 2026-07-24
**Task:** find primary sources for a corpus of Kazakh official/administrative documents, split into two strata, **NORM** (how to write correctly) and **VOLUME** (samples/templates of real documents), with an error typology (Layer A) and a legal assessment regarding personal data.

An important warning up front: the Kazakh official business register (кеңсе тілі / ресми іс қағаздар стилі) is a **low-resource register** that is almost absent from open web text in a clean, normatively checked form. A large part of this report is an honest record of the "white space": where there is no source, I say so directly.

---

## 1. NORM: authoritative sources on how to write official Kazakh correctly

### 1.1. Textbooks and manuals on «іс қағаздарын жүргізу» (records management)

**(a) L. Düisembekova. «Іс қағаздарын қазақша жүргізу».** Almaty: «Ана тілі», 2008. 208 pp.
- ISBN 9965-670-72-2 (2008 edition). There are earlier reprints of the same length (208 pp.) from the same publisher: 2007, ISBN 9965-670-66-8; 2004, ISBN 9965-670-18-8.
- One of the most frequently cited named manuals on Kazakh records management. The bibliographic data is confirmed by the electronic catalogues of Kazakhstani libraries.
- Where to get it: National Academic Library of the RK (nabrk.kz), university libraries; print edition by «Ана тілі».
- Confirmation: nabrk.kz electronic catalogue (see Sources).

**(b) «Мемлекеттік тілде іс қағаздарын жүргізу».** Pavlodar: Toraighyrov University (TOU), 2021. Electronic textbook.
- Available online in full text: https://www.textbook.tou.edu.kz/books/003/intro.html
- Structure: 4 sections: history of the language of business papers, history of the Kazakh press, archival materials, practical drafting of documents in the state language. Intended for universities and colleges.
- Limitation: the intro page does not technically display the authors and ISBN/LBC (there is an «Авторлар» link, but the list is empty in the accessible part); this must be checked in the TOU catalogue. **But the full-text online textbook itself is a valuable normative source; reference wording can be taken from it.**

**(c) Ә. О. Әбутәліп, М. М. Жағыпарова. «Мемлекеттік тілде іс қағаздарын жүргізу».**
- Appears as teaching material (notes/lectures available at studfile.net/preview/12142733/). Full publication details (publisher, year, ISBN) could not be confirmed in a primary catalogue: **white space** (see section 6).

**(d) «Ісқағаздарын жүргізу: оқу құралы».** ISBN 9786010405042 (the 601-04 prefix points to «Қазақ университеті» / Al-Farabi KazNU, Almaty).
- A record with this ISBN was found (dokumen.pub/9786010405042.html and the nabrk catalogue), but at the time of checking the dokumen.pub page was "under maintenance", and full metadata (author/year) was not extracted. The ISBN is valid and citable; details to be checked in the KazNU catalogue.

**Practical conclusion for 1.1:** there is at least one solidly citable manual with an ISBN (Düisembekova) and one full-text online textbook (TOU 2021). That is enough to set the "reference norm". The full bibliographic apparatus for (c) and (d) needs more work through university catalogues.

### 1.2. Kazakhstani document formatting standards (ST RK / GOST)

**(a) ST RK 1042-2001 «Ұйымдастырушылық-өкімдік құжаттама. Құжаттарды ресімдеуге қойылатын талаптар» (Organisational and administrative documentation. Requirements for document formatting).**
- **STATUS: NO LONGER IN FORCE (repealed) since 1 August 2011** (based on a letter of the Committee for Information and Archives dated 28.07.2011).
- This was the key standard on the set and formatting of document details for organisational and administrative documents (resolutions, orders, decisions, minutes, acts, letters). A direct replacement standard with the same name (e.g. "ST RK 1042-2013") **does not exist**; the search confirmed there is no current version.
- Text (for historical cross-checking of document details): prg.kz/document/?doc_id=1051830; the standard's page in the KazStandard shop: new-shop.ksm.kz/catalog/STRK_1042-2001/.
- **Important for the corpus:** despite the repeal, ST RK 1042-2001 remains a useful description of the set of document details (реквизиттер) in Kazakh administrative documents, but it must not be cited as a *current* norm.

**(b) The ST RK ISO 15489 series «Ақпарат және құжаттама. Құжаттаманы басқару» (Information and documentation. Records management).**
- ST RK ISO 15489-1-2007 (general) and ST RK ISO 15489-2-2007 (guidelines) are the Kazakhstani adaptation of ISO 15489. They govern records management in state and commercial organisations; they do not cover archival storage in archival institutions.
- Related adaptations: ST RK ISO 22310-2010 (guidelines for drafting records management standards), ST RK GOST R ISO 23081-1-2010 (metadata for records).
- Issuing body: the state standardisation system of the RK (Committee for Technical Regulation and Metrology / KazStandard). Purchase through the KazStandard shop (ksm.kz); the texts are paid.

**(c) Note on the issuing body.** ST RK standards are issued under the authorised standardisation body (historically "Gosstandart RK" → Committee for Technical Regulation and Metrology). Registry and sales: KazStandard (ksm.kz / new-shop.ksm.kz). eGov does not publish ST RK texts separately.

### 1.3. Official methodological/normative rules on documentation

**(a) The CURRENT main document: «Мемлекеттік және мемлекеттік емес ұйымдарда құжаттама жасаудың, құжаттаманы басқарудың және электрондық құжат айналымы жүйелерін пайдаланудың қағидалары» (Rules for documentation, records management and the use of electronic document management systems in state and non-state organisations).**
- Approved by **Government Decree of the RK No. 703 of 31 October 2018.** In force since 7 December 2018.
- Adopted on the basis of the RK Law of 22.12.1998 "On the National Archival Fund and Archives".
- Official text: the «Әділет» legal information system, adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/P1800000703 (Kazakh and Russian versions available).
- **This is the current methodological basis** for the set and formatting of documents, document details and document flow. It, not the repealed ST RK 1042, is what to rely on as the current "norm for document details".

**(b) Obsolete predecessor (for history):** "Basic rules for documentation and records management…", Resolution of the Cabinet of Ministers of the RK of 30.06.1992 No. 562, **no longer in force** (Government Decree of the RK of 26.02.2003 No. 204). Do not use as a norm.

**(c) Institutional owners of the topic:** the National Archive of the RK and the authorised body for archives and documentation (in different years, the Ministry of Culture and Information / the Committee for Information and Archives). Framework law: "On the National Archival Fund and Archives" (22.12.1998).

## 2. VOLUME: public samples and templates of Kazakh documents (үлгі)

For each core type below: where to actually get samples **in Kazakh**, and in what form they are available.

### 2.1. өтініш (application)
- **University websites are the cleanest source of context** (өтініш деканның/ректордың атына), e.g. академиялық демалыс (academic leave).
  - Serikbayev EKTU (ektu.kz): «Академиялық демалысқа шығу туралы өтініш», in the student service centre section.
  - KazATU (old.kazatu.edu.kz): «Академиялық демалысты рәсімдеуге өтініш беру».
  - KBTU (kbtu.edu.kz/images/514-1-22_2024_kaz.pdf): regulations with application forms.
- **Normative appendix forms:** public service standards on adilet.zan.kz (e.g. V1700015647 «академиялық демалыс беру», which contains an appendix form).
- **Sample portals:** resmihat.kz, zharar.com have өтініш, but as *filled-in examples* (see §4).

### 2.2. түсініктеме (explanatory note)
- Sample portals: resmihat.kz (түсініктеме section), myagro.kz (manual «Мінездеме, өтініш, қолхат жазу үлгісі»).
- HR resources: kadry.mcfr.kz, memos/explanatory notes under the Labour Code of the RK.
- Structure (from these sources): organisation details, document type, date, addressee, text, signature; handwritten on a plain sheet.

### 2.3. қызметтік хат (official letter)
- resmihat.kz («хаттар»/қызметтік хат section): a typology of letters.
- kadry.mcfr.kz «ҚР хаттардың түрлері»: types of letters and how to write them.
- Normative basis for formatting: Rules No. 703 (see §1.3) + the letter series in ST RK 1042 (historical).

### 2.4. бұйрық (order)
- resmihat.kz: samples of HR orders (hiring/transfer/dismissal) under the new Labour Code of the RK.
- kadrykz.kz, kadry.mcfr.kz: personnel orders.
- **The most "normative" examples of бұйрық** are the texts of real orders by state bodies published on GOV.KZ (the unified platform of state bodies' websites, gov.kz) in the "Documents" sections of akimats/agencies. These are public normative/non-normative acts, not personal documents, so a relatively safe source (but check for full names).

### 2.5. мінездеме (character reference)
- zharar.com/kz/4034-minezdeme.html: 3 detailed examples (technical specialist; research fellow; student athlete), in Kazakh with a Russian translation, with formatting instructions (A4, ≤1 page). **These are filled-in samples** (see §4).
- resmihat.kz (мінездеме section), myagro.kz (manual).

### 2.6. арыз (petition / complaint)
- **eOtinish.kz** (eotinish.kz): the state platform for citizens' official appeals to state bodies. It is not a library of ready-made forms but an **interactive form** for submitting an appeal (арыз/шағым). Useful as a source of the *structure* of an official appeal, but no sample text can be downloaded.
- el.kz/tinish_16711/: an article analysing «ӨТІНІШ».
- Sample portals (resmihat.kz, zharar.com): filled-in examples of арыз/өтініш.

### 2.7. General aggregators of Kazakh document samples
- **resmihat.kz/documents/**: the largest thematic catalogue: өтініш, өмірбаян, резюме, бұйрық, мінездеме, түсініктеме, анықтама (certificate), акт, хаттама (minutes), қызметтік хат, келісім (agreement). Format: **filled-in samples** (some with fictitious data).
- **zharar.com**: Kazakh-Russian samples (мінездеме, өмірбаян, түйіндеме, хат and others).
- **kitaphana.kz/deloproizvodstvo.html**: "Samples of records management documents".
- **myagro.kz**: the electronic manual «Мемлекеттік тілде іс қағаздарын жүргізу» with a «жазу үлгісі» section.

### 2.8. eGov.kz: an important clarification
The **eGov.kz** portal does **NOT provide downloadable forms but interactive online services**: a service is filled in through a web form and signed with an EDS (NCA RK), after which it goes to the state body. Appeals go through eOtinish.kz. So eGov is a poor source of "blank templates as files" but a good source of the *set of mandatory fields* for a specific public service. This is a separate "white space": clean downloadable templates of Kazakh documents are practically absent from eGov.

## 3. Error typology (Layer A): what is already documented

### 3.1. In records management manuals and in linguistics of the official style
- **Calques from Russian («орыс тілінің әсері»)** are the most documented class. The thesis: when writing Kazakh texts in the scientific/official business/journalistic style, authors "mould the thought on the Russian pattern" without using the lexical and grammatical resources of Kazakh, so the text turns into a set of redundant calqued constructions.
  - Lexical calques: the review «Қазақ мәтіндеріндегі калька мәселесі: лексикалық калька» (ortalyq.kz).
  - Practical examples of incorrect calques: «үлкен рахмет», «жылы киім» (the norm is «қалың/жұқа киім»).
  - A popular but illustrative analysis of frequent errors: caravan.kz (secondary; for examples, not for the norm).
- **Official register/stylistic errors** and **missing mandatory document details (реквизиттер)** are described in the manuals themselves (Düisembekova; TOU 2021) and set normatively by Rules No. 703 and (historically) ST RK 1042. I did not find a dedicated academic "typology of document-detail errors" in the open: **white space**.

### 3.2. In academic work on Kazakh GEC / spell-checking / morphological analysis

- **Baitenova, Tussupova, Mambetov, Munaitbas, Mukhamejanova (2025). «Hybrid artificial intelligence architectures for automatic text correction in the Kazakh language».** *Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence*, 2025.
  - Error classes (agglutinative morphology): long affix chains (~31–34% of errors), morpheme segmentation errors, loanwords (~14.9%), morphological homonymy without context.
  - Corpus **KazMorphCorpus-2025**: 150,000 sentences / ~2M tokens, 5 genres (fiction, news, social media, Wikipedia, conversational), Cohen's κ = 0.881.
  - Methods: FST (81.5%) → CRF (89%) → KazRoBERTa (final 92.3%).
  - **The most relevant recent work on a typology of Kazakh morphological errors.**

- **«Spelling Correction for Kazakh»** (entry on academia.edu; the page returns 403, metadata not extracted). According to secondary descriptions, the method combines morphological analysis with a noisy-channel model. Needs verification against the primary venue: **partial white space**.

- **Rakhimova D. et al. (Al-Farabi KazNU):**
  - «Development and Study of an Approach for Determining Incorrect Words of the Kazakh Language in Semi-structured Data», ICCCI 2021 (Springer, LNCS): link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-88113-9_43. Detection of incorrect words in semi-structured data.
  - A paper on identifying morphological errors in Kazakh words on social media (2022), mentioned in the ResearchGate profile; exact publication details need checking.

- **«Error Taxonomy and Benchmark Framework for Kazakh Morphological Analyzers Annotation»** (Global Conference on Social Sciences, proudpen.com/…/1006): directly about an **error taxonomy** for Kazakh morphological analysers. Useful for the Layer A annotation scheme.

- **Morphological analysers (infrastructure for rules and case agreement):**
  - «A Uniform Morphological Analyzer for the Kazakh and Turkish Languages» (the Apertium/HFST line).
  - «Two-Level Kazakh Morphology»; «Towards a Data-driven Morphological Analysis of Kazakh Language». They provide a formal apparatus for affixes and cases (септік жалғау), i.e. a basis for checking **case agreement** and **affix errors**.

- **Related ISSAI resources (Nazarbayev University)**: not GEC, but relevant infrastructure: KazNERD (NER, 25 classes), KazParC (parallel corpus), KazSAnDRA (sentiment), KazQAD (QA). **None of them is a dedicated open Kazakh GEC corpus for official language**; this is the key white space.

**Coverage of the requested error classes:**
| Error class | Who documents it |
|---|---|
| Morphology / affix chains | Baitenova 2025 (Frontiers); morphological analysers (Apertium/HFST) |
| Case agreement (септік) | Indirectly, morphological analysers; no dedicated GEC corpus for Kazakh case agreement **found** (white space) |
| Calques from Russian | Linguistics of the official style (ortalyq.kz etc.); manuals |
| Official register / stylistic | Manuals (Düisembekova, TOU 2021) |
| Missing mandatory document details | Rules No. 703; ST RK 1042 (historical). No academic typology (white space) |

## 4. LEGAL: personal data (CRITICAL)

### 4.1. Applicable law
**RK Law «Дербес деректер және оларды қорғау туралы» (On Personal Data and Their Protection) of 21 May 2013 No. 94-V** (as amended, current 2026 version).
- Official text: online.zakon.kz/Document/?doc_id=31396173 (Kazakh/Russian versions).
- Practical consequence for the corpus: full name, IIN (ЖСН), address, date of birth and signature are дербес деректер (personal data). Collecting and processing **real filled-in documents** with such data without a legal basis and the data subject's consent is not permitted.

### 4.2. Classification of the sources found by personal-data risk

| Source | Type | Personal-data risk | Suitability for a public dataset |
|---|---|---|---|
| ST RK 1042 / Rules No. 703 / ST RK ISO 15489 | Description of document details, empty schemas | None | Safe (normative basis) |
| Textbooks (Düisembekova, TOU 2021) | Teaching samples, often fictitious | Low (check for real names) | Conditionally safe; check |
| resmihat.kz, zharar.com, kitaphana.kz, myagro.kz | **Filled-in samples** (often fictitious names, but realistic ones occur) | **Medium** | Only after checking and anonymisation; see licences below |
| University forms (ektu, kazatu, kbtu) | Appendix forms / regulations | Low (the forms themselves are empty) | Safe as **empty templates with placeholders** |
| adilet.zan.kz (public service appendix forms) | Empty normative forms | None | Safe |
| eGov.kz / eOtinish.kz | Interactive forms (field structure) | None (the form itself is empty) | Safe as structure; no sample texts |
| GOV.KZ (orders of state bodies) | Real acts | **Variable**: personnel orders contain full names | Take only anonymised/normative ones, filter names |

### 4.3. Practical rule (supported by the nature of the sources)
1. **Collect empty templates/forms (үлгі) with placeholders**, not filled-in documents.
2. **Anonymise before ingestion into the corpus, not after.** Even "fictitious" samples from portals must go through a name/IIN/address filter.
3. Do **not include** real filled-in documents (explanatory notes, references, applications of specific people) in the public dataset.

### 4.4. Licensing / redistribution
- **resmihat.kz**: the footer says "All rights reserved" © 2026, and there are "Terms of use". No explicit open licence (CC) → **redistribution of the samples is not legally permitted by default**; use as a structural reference, not as republishable content.
- **zharar.com**: no explicit licence terms on the page; the site presents itself as a free educational portal, but the absence of a licence ≠ permission to redistribute.
- **eGov.kz / eOtinish.kz**: state resources; portal terms of use; no open licence for "templates" (and there are no template files there anyway).
- **adilet.zan.kz**: the official legal portal; texts of legal acts (including appendix forms) are public domain in the sense of normative acts; the "cleanest" source for document details and empty forms.
- **Conclusion:** for a legally clean corpus, rely on (1) normative forms from adilet.zan.kz and university regulations and (2) self-synthesised anonymised templates based on the document details described in the manuals. Content from commercial sample portals is only a structural reference, not republishable data.

## 5. Overall assessment of the strata
- **NORM** is covered with confidence: a citable manual with an ISBN (Düisembekova), a full-text online textbook (TOU 2021), the current Rules No. 703, the historical ST RK 1042 and the ST RK ISO 15489 series, and the current Personal Data Law No. 94-V.
- **VOLUME** is covered only in fragments: samples exist, but they are either filled in (personal-data risk + licences) or interactive forms with no downloadable text. **No** clean openly licensed corpora of Kazakh official documents **were found**; this confirms the niche nature of the task.

---

## 6. What remains unclear / white space

1. **No open CC-licensed corpus of official Kazakh exists** (as found). We will have to synthesise/anonymise it ourselves.
2. **There are practically no downloadable empty templates on eGov.kz**: the portal is built on interactive forms + EDS, not on handing out forms as files.
3. **Full bibliographic data for some manuals is not confirmed in a primary catalogue:** Әбутәліп/Жағыпарова (publisher/year/ISBN); the textbook with ISBN 9786010405042 (author/year; dokumen.pub was under maintenance); the authors and ISBN of the TOU 2021 online textbook were not shown on the intro page.
4. **There is no dedicated academic GEC corpus for the Kazakh official business register.** KazMorphCorpus-2025, KazNERD and the like are general or mixed-genre, not officialese.
5. **No separate academic typology of "document-detail errors" or of case agreement specifically in business documents was found**: only general morphology and the general linguistics of calques. This is a gap your project can close itself.
6. **«Spelling Correction for Kazakh» (academia.edu) is not verified** against its primary venue (page 403). Access through a library/the ACL Anthology is needed for the exact authors/year.
7. **There is no current replacement standard with the same name as ST RK 1042.** The norm for document details has to be assembled from Rules No. 703 + ST RK ISO 15489 (paid texts) + the historical ST RK 1042.
